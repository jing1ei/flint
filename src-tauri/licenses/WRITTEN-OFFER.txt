WHAT THIS COVERS
----------------

Flint ships two prebuilt binaries inside the application bundle,
`ffmpeg` and `ffprobe`. They are not our work and they are not MIT: they are
covered by the GNU General Public License. Handing you the .dmg or the .app.zip is
redistribution of a GPL work, which obliges us to give you the licence text (it is
in this same folder) and to make the corresponding source available to you.

Which GPL version applies depends on how the binary in *your* download was
configured, and it differs between the two architectures. The file FFMPEG-BUILD.txt
next to this one records the exact `ffmpeg -version` output, including the full
configure line, for the copy you have, and names the licence file that applies. Read
that file first; everything below refers to the build it describes.

Flint's own code is proprietary and is not covered by this offer. It
runs FFmpeg as a separate process and does not link against it. Its complete source
is public at __REPOSITORY_URL__.

HOW TO GET THE SOURCE
---------------------

1. It is already attached to your release. Every published release carries the
   matching upstream FFmpeg source archive next to the .dmg files, named after the
   exact version the binaries report, e.g. `ffmpeg-<version>-source-for-<arch>.tar.xz`.
   Release page: __RELEASE_URL__

2. Ask us. Open an issue titled "GPL source request" at
   __ISSUE_URL__
   and paste the `ffmpeg version ...` line from FFMPEG-BUILD.txt (or from
   Contents/Resources/licenses/FFMPEG-BUILD.txt inside the app). The
   Flint maintainers monitor that tracker and answer source requests there;
   that issue tracker is the contact route for this offer. If a public tracker does
   not suit you, say so in an issue and we will arrange another channel.

3. Upstream, independently of us and permanently:
   https://ffmpeg.org/download.html          release tarballs
   https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git         canonical git
   https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg          official mirror


WHERE THIS OFFER IS IMPERFECT, STATED PLAINLY
---------------------------------------------

We would rather tell you the limits of what we can supply than imply a completeness
we cannot back up.

* We did not compile these binaries. They are community builds: osxexperts.net for
  Apple Silicon, evermeet.cx for Intel. The source we attach is the corresponding
  *upstream* FFmpeg source for the version string the binary reports, taken from
  ffmpeg.org (or, for a snapshot build, the upstream commit the version string
  names).

* If the publisher applied patches on top of that upstream tree, we do not have
  them and we cannot give them to you. We do not claim that the attached tarball
  compiles bit-for-bit into the binary you received. What we can give you is the
  publisher's complete `configure` line, recorded verbatim in FFMPEG-BUILD.txt, so
  that the build is reproducible in principle from the attached source.

* GPL-2.0 section 3 also asks for "the scripts used to control compilation and
  installation". FFmpeg is configured by that one `configure` invocation, which is
  why we record it in full; we do not hold the publishers' own build harnesses and
  do not pretend to.

* The publishers' own pages are not a source offer we can pass on to you.
  osxexperts.net describes its binaries as being "for educational purposes only",
  its License link serves the Apache-2.0 licence of its build scripts rather than
  FFmpeg's GPL, and its "source used to compile" link has pointed at an FFmpeg
  branch much older than the binary beside it. That is exactly why this offer, the
  attached tarball and the recorded configure line exist rather than a link to them.

* If you find that the source we attached does not correspond to the binary you
  received, that is a bug in our compliance and we want to hear about it: open an
  issue as in (2). The fix on our side is to rebuild from a build we control, and we
  will do that rather than leave the discrepancy standing.
